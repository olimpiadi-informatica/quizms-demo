Tip-Tap has to prepare lunch and has lined up $N$ foods to eat.
Each food is a vegetable or a fruit, and Tip-Tap only wants to eat **raw fruit** and **cooked vegetables**.
You can use these blocks:

- `N`: the number of foods Tip-Tap has lined up.
- `next food`: the next food that Tip-Tap hasn't eaten yet.
- $x$ `is a vegetable`: true if food $x$ is a vegetable.
- `cook` $x$: cook food $x$.
- `eat` $x$: eat food $x$.
- `finish`: stop eating.

Write a program that lets Tip-Tap have lunch the way it likes best!
